Our Story

Welcome to Calabria
Pizzeria

At Calabria Pizzeria, we're committed to serving the North Bellmore community with authentic Italian pizza and dishes made from the finest ingredients. Our recipes are inspired by traditional Italian cooking, bringing genuine flavor to every bite.

Whether you're craving a classic slice of pizza, fresh pasta, or Italian specialties, we prepare everything with care and attention to detail. We pride ourselves on offering quality food at fair prices, making authentic Italian cuisine accessible to our community.

We offer delivery, takeout, and catering for all occasions. Thank you for supporting Calabria Pizzeria — we look forward to serving you and becoming your go-to spot for delicious Italian food.

— The Team at Calabria
